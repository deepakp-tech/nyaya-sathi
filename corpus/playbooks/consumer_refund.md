## Evidence to collect
- Invoice/order ID, payment proof, product photos/videos (unboxing if available)
- Complaint tickets, emails, chat transcripts with the seller/platform
- Warranty card, delivery date, any written refusal

## Steps, cheapest first
1. Written complaint to the company's grievance officer (e-commerce sites must list one). Note the ticket number.
2. Register on the National Consumer Helpline (1915 / website / app). Free; often resolves refunds.
3. Legal notice to the company (optional but strengthens the case), 15-day deadline.
4. File a complaint at the District Consumer Commission via e-Daakhil. No lawyer needed. Check the 2-year limit.

## What you can ask for
- Refund, replacement, repair, compensation for loss/harassment, litigation costs.

## When to get a lawyer
- High-value claims (State/National Commission level), medical negligence, builder/real-estate disputes, or the company files a counter-case.
