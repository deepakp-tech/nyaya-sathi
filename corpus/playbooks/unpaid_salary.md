## Evidence to collect
- Offer/appointment letter, payslips, bank statements showing past salary credits
- Resignation email and acceptance, relieving letter (if any), notice-period dates
- Attendance/work proof for unpaid months, HR emails and messages

## Steps, cheapest first
1. Written email to HR and a director/founder listing exact dues and a deadline.
2. Legal notice demanding salary + full and final settlement within 15 days.
3. Complaint to the state Labour Commissioner/authority (check if your role is covered).
4. Civil suit for recovery (often the route for managers/senior staff).
5. If the company is insolvent, dues may be claimed in insolvency proceedings — get a lawyer.

## When to get a lawyer
- Company has shut down or is insolvent, large amounts, the employer alleges misconduct, or the claim is close to 3 years old.
